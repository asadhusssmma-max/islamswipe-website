Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026

Introduction

IslamSwipe ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Data (Apple Sign In)

When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple user ID and, optionally, your name and email address if you choose to share them. We also store your display name, username, bio, and profile photo that you voluntarily provide.

1.2 Account Data (Guest)

Guest users do not provide any personal information. A local anonymous session is created on your device. Guest data (preferences, dhikr sessions, goals) is stored locally and is not transmitted to our servers unless you later create an account.

1.3 Usage Data

We collect data about your activity within the App, including likes, saves, dhikr session counts, Quran reading progress, goals, follower/following relationships, and content preferences. This data is used to personalise your experience.

1.4 Subscription Data

Subscription status is managed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not store your payment information — all transactions are processed by Apple's App Store. We receive only your subscription status (active, expired, or trial) and the anonymous RevenueCat app user ID.

1.5 Device Tokens

We collect anonymous device tokens for push notification delivery via Apple Push Notification Service. These tokens are used solely to send you reminders and motivational notifications. We do not collect device model, OS version, or IP address for tracking purposes.

1.6 Creator Content

If you are an approved creator, we collect and store video files, thumbnails, captions, hashtags, and category tags that you upload. Creator metadata such as creator bio, content style, and tier level is also stored.

1.7 Comments and Social Interactions

Comments you post on content, reports you submit, and users you block are stored to enable social features and content moderation. Follower/following relationships are stored to deliver personalised feeds.

1.8 Data Export

You can export a copy of your personal data at any time using the "Download My Data" option in Account Settings. The export is a JSON file containing your profile information (including subscription status, but never payment details), your activity history (dhikr sessions, goals, Quran reading progress, comments you have posted, reports you have submitted, accounts you follow and who follows you, and accounts you have blocked or muted), your preferences (content filters, struggles, custom dhikrs, your comment discussion preference, and notification settings), your uploaded posts if you are a creator, and your saved and liked content.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

IslamSwipe does not collect:

  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Camera or microphone data — the App does not access your camera or microphone
  • Health or fitness data
  • Biometric data (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprints)
  • Browsing history or data from other apps
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Any data from third-party analytics or tracking SDKs

We do not use any advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or sell your data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Apple Sign In: Authentication is handled by Apple. We receive only the data you authorise Apple to share. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/privacy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and subscription transaction data from Apple. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Supabase: Provides our backend database, authentication, and file storage services (including creator video uploads). Data is hosted on AWS infrastructure. See Supabase's privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.
  • Cloudflare Workers: Hosts our secure server endpoints, which verify subscription status and process account deletion requests. Data passes through Cloudflare's network but is not stored there. See Cloudflare's privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • Apple Push Notification Service: Delivers push notifications to your device using an anonymous device token.
  • Quran.com API: We stream Quran audio recitations from verses.quran.com. When you play Quran audio, your device connects directly to their servers. See Quran.com's privacy policy at quran.com/privacy.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • App Functionality: To provide and personalise the IslamSwipe experience, including your feed, dhikr tracking, Quran progress, and saved content.
  • Notifications: To send you reminders and motivational notifications based on your activity and preferences. You can disable these at any time in Settings.
  • Subscriptions: To verify your premium subscription status and unlock premium features.
  • Social Features: To enable followers, following, creator discovery, and profile visibility. When you follow a creator, their posts appear in your Following feed.
  • Creator Program: To enable approved creators to upload, manage, and distribute Islamic content to their followers.
  • Content Moderation: To review creator-submitted content for quality and adherence to Islamic content standards before it is made visible to followers.
  • Improvements: To understand general usage patterns and improve the App. We do not use personal data for advertising.

5. Content Moderation

IslamSwipe operates a content moderation system for creator-submitted content. New creators' posts are placed in a review queue and must be manually approved before becoming visible to followers. Once a creator demonstrates consistent quality, they may be granted auto-publish privileges at our discretion.

We reserve the right to review, reject, remove, or restrict any content at any time without prior notice. Users can report content or other users that violate our standards using the in-app reporting feature. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours and appropriate action is taken, which may include content removal or account suspension.

6. Legal Basis (UK GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following legal bases as defined by the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR):

  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide you with the App's services, including account management, content delivery, and subscription management.
  • Legitimate Interests: Processing necessary for our legitimate interests, including improving the App, ensuring security, and preventing abuse, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: Processing based on your explicit consent, such as sending push notifications and optional data sharing during Apple Sign In. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal Obligation: Processing necessary to comply with legal obligations, such as responding to lawful requests from authorities.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our servers, including:

  • Profile information (name, username, bio, photo)
  • Activity history (likes, saves, sessions, progress)
  • Uploaded content (videos, thumbnails, captions)
  • Comments and reports
  • Follower and following relationships
  • Preferences and settings

Local data on your device (preferences, cached content) can be cleared manually from the Profile tab or by uninstalling the App.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access: You can view your data in the Profile tab at any time.
  • Export: You can download a copy of your data using the "Download My Data" option in Account Settings.
  • Rectification: You can update your profile information at any time through the App.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data from Account Settings. Guest users can clear all local data from the Profile tab.
  • Restriction: You can request restriction of processing by contacting us.
  • Objection: You can object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting us.
  • Portability: You can export your data in a machine-readable JSON format.
  • Notifications: You can disable all notifications from the Notifications settings within the App or from iOS Settings.
  • Content Removal: Creators can delete their own uploaded posts at any time from the My Posts section.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at islamswipeapp@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

IslamSwipe is intended for users aged 13 and over, in line with our Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App's content is educational and religious in nature, and users under 18 should use the App with parental guidance. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Data Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure) with encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Local data such as preferences, dhikr sessions, and goals are stored on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including secure API communication, row-level security policies, and access controls.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States (via AWS infrastructure used by Supabase) and other jurisdictions where our service providers operate. Requests to our server endpoints are routed through Cloudflare's global edge network and may be processed at a location outside your country of residence; as stated in Section 3, your data is not stored there. Where data is transferred outside the UK or EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including standard contractual clauses and adequacy decisions, to protect your personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
